PAGE 1
Shri Shaunak Maharshi says:
1. Shaunak Maharshigalu Suta Puranikarannu prashne maaduttare. Elai Sutane ninu Mahaprajna. Samasta shastragala tattvarthagalannu spashtavagi tilidiruvavanu. Adara karanadinda nanage Puranagala kathasaaravannu visheshavagi vivarisi tilisu.
2. Shreshthavada punyavoo olleya daivabhaktiyoo, itanta karya pravruttavaguvantaha vivekavoo hege abhivruddhi honduttade. Manassina kalmashavannu nasha madikolluvikeyoo sajjanarinda hege madalpaduttade endu prashne.

PAGE 2
3. Ella yugagaliginta ghoravada ee Kaliyugadalli prayah ella jeevigalu rakshasatvavannu hondi, tamogunapradhanavagi dushta svabhavadavaru. Idu hogalisi avarannu sanmargadalli pravartisalu uttama margavideye endu prashnisuttare. Gunagalu mooru — sattva, rajo, tamoguna. Sattvagunayuktaru shanti, samyama, satkaryanishtharagi uttamaragiruttare. Rajogunayuktaru nyayanishthuragi, shiksharharige matra shikshe needutta, sajjanarannu aadarisi, madhyama svabhavadavaragiruttare. Tamogunadhikaru rakshasarante sajjana-durjanara bhedavillade ellarannoo karanavilladiddaroo himsisutta bhayankaragiruttare.
4. Mele helida reetiyalliruva janaru sanmarga pravruttaragalu ee prapanchadalli shreyassendu yavudideyo adakkintaloo uttamavada shreyassu, hagu pavitravadudu yavudideyo adakkintaloo pavitravada sadhanegalannu nanage iga helu. 'Shreyanam shreyah' emballi gadhavada arthavide. Kathopanishattinalli Yama-Nachiketa samvadadalli shreyassina vicharavannu Yama devanu vivarisiddane. Preyassu mattu shreyassu eradu vidha. Preyassu yajna-dana-tapa-japadi karma, shastra vihita kamya karmagalinda doreyuva svargadi ihika phalavulladdu. Shreyassu shashvatavada jnana-rupada moksha-phalavulladdu; janma-maranarahita mokshavannu needuvantahude shreyassu.

PAGE 3
5. Yava antaha shreyahpradavad sadhanadinda begane nirmala manassullavanagi atma-shuddhiyannu hondi nantara Shivapraptiyannu padeyalu sadhyavada sadhanavannu nanage elai poojyane tilisu. Mokshapraptige chittashuddhi avashya embudannu matte tilisidantayitu.
6. Sutaru heluttare. Elai munishreshthana nine dhanyanu; Shiv sambandhavada shastravannu kelabekemba preeti, aadaragalullavanagiruve. Adarinda chennagi alochisi atyanta uttamavada shastravannu heluve. Satkarmadalli, sachchastradalli aasaktiyu aneka punyainda labhyavaguvantahadu. Shastra endare 'shastri iti shastra' — heege madabekendu vidhisuvude shastra.
7. Samastavada shastra-puranagalalli antim teermanaveenideyo, avugala mooloddesha-phalaveenideyo avugala saaravannu olagondiruva, bhakti-jnana-vairagyagalannu visheshaagi abhivruddhigolisuvantaha, Shivapreetikaravada madhuravada ee Shivapurana vannu elai vatsane kelu endu heluttare.

PAGE 4
8. Kalavemba mahasarpa-bhayadinda koodiruva manushyarige mahabhayavannu nivarisuvantaha uttama, hinde Ishvaranindale upadeshisalpatt 'Shiva' Puranavannu heluttEne.
9. Sanatkumara munigalu atyanta aadaradinda ee Puranavannu Vyasarge upadeshisidaru. Hage upadesha hondida Vyasaru Kaliyugada janara eligegoskara heliddare.
10. Antaha ee Shivapuranakkinta shreshthavada matt ondu Puranavilla. Adaralloo ee Kaliyugadalli janarige manashuddhiyuntumadalu idakkinta shreshthavadaddu yavudoo illa. Shivapurana pathana-shravana atyanta mukhya sadhaneyagide.
11. Shivapurana shravanadinda ee janmadalloo mundina janmagalloo Shivanalli bhaktiyuntagi mahapunyavoo labhisuvudu. Hage mahabhagyashaliyoo aguvanu endu elai muniye Puranavicharagalannu chennagi tilisiruva maharshigala abhipraya.

PAGE 5
12. Ee Shivapurana atyanta pavitrawoo shreshtha shastravoo agide. Ee Shivapurana bhumiyalli sakshattagiruva Shivasvarupave agide. Adarinda Shivasvarupavada ee Shivapuranavannu sarva prayatnadindaloo sevisabeku.
13. Ee shreshtha Shivapuranada keluvike, oduvikeyinda dehanashanantara Shivabhaktiyulla itanta manushyaru Shivasayujyavannu padeyuvaru. Adarinda Shiva-sayujya padeyalu sadhanavada shravana/pathanagalannu madabeku.
14. Adarinda sarva prayatnadindaloo pathanava madabeku. Sarva prayatnadindaloo ee Puranavannu pathana madida manushya samasta kamanegalannu hondvanu. Preetiyinda Puranavannu kelidavanu saha sarva ishtarthagalannu padeyuvanu.
15. Shivapuranada shravanadinda manushyanU samasta papagalinda bidalpadavanu; hagEye bahalada shreshtha sukhavannu anubhavisI koneyalli Shivalokavannu hondvanu.

PAGE 6
16. Rajasuya yaga mattu nooru Agnishtoma yagagalinda siguva mahatta punya Shivapurana shravanadindale labhisuvaudu.
17. Ee lokadalli yaru ee Shiv sambandhavada uttama Shivapuranavannu keluvaro avaru manushyaralla endu tilibeku; antahavaru sakshat ShivasvaruparE. Ee vicharadalli samshayabeda.
18. Ee Puranavannu keluvavara mattu kirtisuvavara padakamalagalu sakala punya tirthagala svarupavendu munigalu heliruvru.
19. Yaru mokshavannu hondalu apeksisuvro antahavaru yavagaloo ee shreshtha Shivapuranavannu bhaktinda kelabeku. Muktige idakkinta shreshtha sadhana berondilla. Mukti endare bidugade — janma-maranagalinda bidalpaddu Shivanalli seruvudu.

PAGE 7
20. Yavagloo ee Shivapuranavannu kelalu sadhyavilladavanu, elai munishreshthane, niyamapoorvakavagi pratidinavoo muhurtakalavadaroo kelabahudu. Muhurta endare 48 nimishagalu.
21. Ondu vele pratidina shravana madalu sadhyavagadiddare, antaha manushya punyakaravada masa, tithi, nakshatragalalli Shivapuranavannu kelabeku. Kartika masa, Ashtami, Chaturdashi, Sankramana, Purnima mattu Shivapreetikara parvakaladalli, Ardra nakshatra dinagalalli Shivapurana shravana Shivaseveyalli uttamavadudu.
22. Ondu muhurtavagali, ardha muhurtavagali, kalu muhurtavagali, ondu kshanavagali yaaru ee shreshtha Shivapuranavannu keluvaro antahavarige durgati untaguvudilla.
23. Yava manushyaru ee Shivapuranavannu keluvaro, munishvarane, antahavaru karmagalemba maharanyavannu suttu, datalashakya samsara samudravannu dati mokshavannu honduttare. Karmagalu nishkama mattu sakama endu eradu vidha.

PAGE 8
24. Ella danagalindaloo, sarva yajnyagalindaloo yava punyavu labhyavaguvudo, elai muniyE, antaha shreshtha punyagallellavoo Shivapuranada shravanadindale nishchayavagi untaguttave.
25. Visheshavagi heluvudadare ee Kaliyugadalli Shivapuranavannu bittu mukti-sadhanegE bere margavilla. Mukti sadhanegE upayuktavada bere dharmavu manushyarige Shivapuranavannu bittu iruvudilla.
26. Shivapurana shravanavoo Shivanama smaraneyoo manushyarige Kalpadrumakke samanavadavu; ivu manushyarige sakala kamanegalannoo kodatakkavugalagive. Idaralli samshayavilla.
27. Ee Kaliyugadalli mandabuddhiyullavaru, dharmachara-rahitaru itantahavaru sadgatiyannu hondalende Shivapurana emba amrutavannu untu madiruvanu.
28. Amruta panamadi davanobbanE muppu-savugalilladavanuvanu; adare Shivapurana emba amrutapanamadidavanu tanna kulavanne ajaramaravannagi madvanu. Endare tanna kulakke sadhgati untaguvante maduvanu.

PAGE 9
29. Paramapavitravada ee Shivapuranada ella kathegaloo mokshapradavadavu. Adarinda ellarindaloo yavagaloo sevisalpadtakkadaddu. 'Sada sevya' endu mooru bari heliddarinda udasinamade khanditavagi vishesha karyavendu pariganisi sevisabeku.
30. Ee Shivapurana shravanada phalavannu enendu helali? Ee Purana shravana-punyadinda shravana maduvavana manassinalli Shivane sakshattagi vasisuvanu.
31. Ee grantha ippattunalku savira shlokagalinda koodi elu samhitegalinda koodide. Bhakti, jnana, vairagyagalannu tilisuva ee Puranavannu atyanta bhakti-shraddhegalinda kelabeku.
32-33. Ee Puranadalli Vidyeshvara Samhite modalaneyadu, eradaneyadu Rudra Samhita, mooraneyadu Shatarudra Samhita, nalkaneyadu Kotirudra Samhita, aidaneyadu Uma Samhita, araneyadu Kailasa Samhita, elaneyadu Vayaviya Samhita — heege elu Samhitegalive.
34. Ee elu Samhitegalinda koodida shreshtha Shivapurana Parabrahma svarupavadudu. Ella vidhadindaloo shreshtha gatIyannu needuvudu.
35. Ee reetiyA uttama mahimeyulla sapta Samhitegalondigoodida Shivapuranavannu sampoornavagi yavanu bhaktinda oduvano avanu Jivanmukta endu helalpaduttane.

PAGE 10
36. Manushya itanta uttamavada Shivapuranavannu kelade iruvavarege samsaradalli tolaraduttiruttane. Yavaga Shivapurana shravana madvano koodale avanige jnanodaya aguvudu. Jnana-ajnanada sambandha mattu samsara-bandhana/vairagya kuritu ee pustakadalli vivarane ide; idannu source commentaryyalli ulisalagide.
37. Ee prapanchadalli bhrameyannu untumaduvanta aneka shastragaloo Puranagaloo ive. Avara shravanadinda enu prayojana? OndE ondada Shivapurana shravanadinda muktiprapti endu Shivapurana heluttiruvudu.
38. Ee reetiya mahamahimeyulla Shivapurana katheyu yava maneyalli nadeyuvudo antaha maneyu pavitra tirthagalinda koodiruttade. Antaha maneyalli vasisuvavara papagalu nashavaguttave.

PAGE 11
39. Ashvamedha yagagalu savira, Vajapeya yagagalu nooru — ivugalannu acharisuvudarinda siguva phala Shivapurana pathana-phalada hadinaraneya ondu bhagakke samanavaguvudu.
40. Elliyavarege Shivapuranavannu bhaktinda manushyanu keluvudillavo alliyavarege avanu papiyendE helalpaduttane.
41. Gangadi punya nadigaloo, elu punya nagaragaloo, punyakshetragaloo Shivapuranakke sarisamanagalaru. Saptapunya nadigalu: Ganga, Yamuna, Godavari, Sarasvati, Narmada, Sindhu, Kaveri. Sapta mokshadayaka nagaragalannu saha source commentary vivarisuttade.
42. Pratidinavoo Shivapuranada ondu shlokavannagali athava ardha shlokavannagali tanna mukhadinda bhaktiyinda nitya parayanadante pathisuvavanu uttama gati/mokshavannu hondvanu.
43. Ee pavitra Shivapuranavannu yavanu yavagaloo arthanuvada maduvano athava preetiyinda dinavoo oduvano antahavanu punyatmane sari; idralli samshayavilla.

PAGE 12
44. Antyakala, arthat marana kaladalli, yava manushya ee Shivapuranavannu atyanta bhaktinda shravana maduvano antahavanige Ishvara prasannanagi tanna padavannu needuvanu; idu Kailasa praptiyendu arthavendu source commentary heluttade.
45. Yavanu ee Shivapuranavannu pratinityavoo bhakti-shraddhegalinda poojisuvano, antahavanu ee lokadalli anubhavisabekada sukhagalannu anubhavisI kadege Shivasayujyavannu padeyuvanu.
46. Ee Shivapuranavannu yavanu nityavoo shraddheyinda reshme battegalinda satkarisi poojisuvano antahavanu yavagaloo sukhiyaguva.
47. Iha-paragalEradaralliyoo sukhavannu bayasuvavanu jnanaprada Shivapuranavannu, Shiva sambandhavada ella vicharagalannu prayatnapoorvakavagi sevisabeku.
48. Dharma, artha, kama, mokshagalemba nalku purusharthagalannu needuvantaha ee shreshtha Puranavannu sarvada kelabeku. TanE preetiyinda odidare vishesha phalavuntu.
49. Vedagalu, Itihasagalu, sakala shastragalellakkintaloo uttamavagi ee Purana shreyaskara endu source edition heluttade; moksha bayasuvavaru tilidu adhyayana madabeku.

PAGE 13
50. Ee Shaiva Purana atma-tattvavannu tilidavarige shreshthavadaddu. Sakala sajjanarinda yavagaloo uttamavendu sevisalpattu prasiddhavagide. Idannu pathana, shravana maduvavaru adhyatmika, adhidaivika, adhibhautika embha tapatrayagalannu hogalisi sukhavannu padeyuvaru. Brahma, Vishnu, Maheshvara modalada devategalige pranakkintaloo priyavadudu.
51. Itanta mahamahimegalinda koodida ee Shivapuranavannu yavagaloo nanu suprasanna manaskanagi namaskaristtene. Idarinda Shiva nannalli prasannanagi nanage avana padaravindagalalli bhaktiyannu anugrahisali.
